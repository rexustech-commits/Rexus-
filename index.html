About RexusTech

We are an engineering startup focused on bringing structural AI, IoT, and data liquidity to the modern enterprise.

Founded on the principle that technology should solve real-world bottlenecks, RexusTech bridges the gap between hardware and intelligent software.

Our team of engineers specializes in writing custom firmware for edge devices, building scalable cloud architectures, and training bespoke computer vision models that allow businesses to operate faster and smarter.
