Real Products

Rexus Engine

What it does: Adapts and fine-tunes best-in-class foundation models securely for specific enterprise parameters.

Who it's for: Organizations requiring private, locally-hosted LLMs and AI processing.

Key benefit: Verified zero data leaks with low-latency outputs.

NeuroVision Core

What it does: Specialized deep-learning framework for real-time video parsing, object tracking, and defect recognition.

Who it's for: Manufacturing floors and facilities relying on visual quality control.

Key benefit: Eliminates manual visual inspection failures and automates high-resolution data streams.

What it does: An IoT bridge platform connecting localized hardware directly to secure cloud systems.

Who it's for: Industrial plants and enterprises managing disconnected hardware sensor data.

Key benefit: Near-zero latency industrial monitoring and eliminated network bottlenecks.
